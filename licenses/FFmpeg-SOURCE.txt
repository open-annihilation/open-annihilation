They were built unmodified from

  https://ffmpeg.org/releases/ffmpeg-9.0.2.tar.gz
  SHA-256 84960df915059e8754fef2cd7c9afeb614062b1b5458ec471eecee619ee04e98

with these configure options besides the installation --prefix:

  --enable-shared
  --disable-static
  --disable-programs
  --disable-doc
  --disable-debug
  --disable-autodetect
  --disable-network
  --disable-avdevice
  --disable-avfilter
  --disable-x86asm
  --disable-everything
  --enable-protocol=file
  --enable-demuxer=smacker,mp3,ogg,wav,flac
  --enable-decoder=smacker,smackaud,mp3float,vorbis,flac,pcm_s16le,pcm_s24le,pcm_u8
  --enable-parser=mpegaudio,vorbis,flac
  --enable-pthreads
  --install-name-dir=@rpath
  --extra-cflags=-mmacosx-version-min=11.0
  --extra-ldflags=-mmacosx-version-min=11.0

They are separate shared libraries and can be replaced by another compatible
FFmpeg build of the same major versions.
