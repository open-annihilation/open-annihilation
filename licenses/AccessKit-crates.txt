AccessKit C bindings 0.23.2 (crate accesskit-c).

Every crate cargo metadata reports for each target, from the tag's Cargo.lock
(cargo metadata --locked --format-version 1 --filter-platform <triple>).
The resolve includes the crates a source build compiles for that target,
proc-macro crates among them; a proc-macro crate is not linked into the
finished library. The Rust standard library compiled into the libraries is
listed at the end, from rustc 1.99.1's library/Cargo.lock.

The engine takes AccessKit under the MIT licence. Where a crate's licence
expression offers MIT or Apache-2.0, written "OR" or "/", the engine takes
one of those. unicode-ident is (MIT OR Apache-2.0) AND Unicode-DFS-2016.
The Unicode-DFS-2016 text is licenses/AccessKit-Unicode-DFS-2016.txt.
accesskit-c's deny.toml allows that Unicode licence for unicode-ident alone.
No crate on a shipped target is only under a licence outside Apache-2.0,
Apache-2.0 WITH LLVM-exception, BSD-2-Clause, BSD-3-Clause, ISC, MIT, Zlib
and that Unicode-DFS-2016 exception.

The copyright line is the crate's authors field when that field names
someone. When it is empty, the line is the copyright notice in the crate's
own licence file. The objc2 framework crates publish no licence file; their
line is the MIT notice in the objc2 repository that their README links to
(commit e282618be4c3a3b9542957e0c8540e9588472ce8). pin-project-lite and
winnow publish an MIT text that names no copyright holder.

## Rust standard library, rustc 1.99.1

The libraries are built by rustc 1.99.1 (58cf8f9c3 2026-10-13), as each
library's .comment records. That toolchain's standard library is compiled
into every AccessKit library: std, core, alloc, the panic runtime,
compiler_builtins (including compiler-rt's C objects) and the crates those
link. Versions and licences below are from that toolchain's library/Cargo.lock.
A crate that lives in the toolchain tree is Copyright (c) The Rust Project
Contributors. The other copyright lines are from each crate's own licence file.

compiler_builtins also carries compiler-rt. Work derived from compiler-rt
before 2019-01-19 is MIT, Copyright (c) 2009-2016 by the contributors listed
in compiler-rt's CREDITS.TXT. Work after that date is Apache-2.0 WITH
LLVM-exception. The exception text is licenses/AccessKit-LLVM-exception.txt.
The Apache-2.0 text is licenses/AccessKit-Apache-2.0.txt.

Where a crate offers MIT or Apache-2.0, the engine takes one of those.
adler2 also offers 0BSD; the engine takes MIT or Apache-2.0. memchr offers
the Unlicense or MIT; the engine takes MIT. miniz_oxide offers MIT or Zlib
or Apache-2.0; the engine takes MIT or Apache-2.0.

Every package in the lock is listed. A crate marked as not in the libraries
this engine ships is there for tests, a build script, the compiler, or
another operating system. fortanix-sgx-abi is MPL-2.0 and is one of those:
it is not compiled into a library this engine ships, and the engine does
not take that licence. r-efi offers LGPL-2.1-or-later as well as MIT or
Apache-2.0; it is UEFI only and is not compiled in. The engine would take
MIT or Apache-2.0.
