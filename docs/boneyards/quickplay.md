# QuickPlay

QuickPlay is the search on the Main Frame. It is open to an admin or a beta
tester. Anyone else is told that QuickPlay is not open for this account.

The console asks for a game, where to look, a skill, a side and, if wanted,
the favorite map, then FIND BATTLE starts the search at once. CANCEL SEARCH
leaves it. Closing the console while a search is running leaves it too.

The first line says how many planets are being scanned, or that the search
is on Fuel Dump. Further lines appear one at a time, naming a planet that
had nothing, a battle that was skipped, or a battle that fits. The queue
meter shows how many battles fit. The timer and the scanner stay with the
search.

When a battle fits, the offer asks "Join now?". Yes joins it. No stays on
the search, and that battle is not offered again. When nothing fits, the
offer asks "No games available, host one?". Yes hosts that battle with the
numbers the offer carries. No stays on the search.

Yes ends the search, as the battle it starts leaves the queue: back from
the battle room, the console shows FIND BATTLE. When the battle is refused
(it filled, or it could not be hosted), the queue goes on searching, and
the console goes back to the search when the queue says so.

A battle started on a planet other than the one the Main Frame has selected
is left on the planet the launch named. The settings, the players and the
score of that battle go out for that planet too.

A search the queue will not take returns to the settings and says why: the
battle is not allowed on that planet, the commander is already in a battle,
Fuel Dump is not in this sector, the queue dropped the search, or the
settings cannot be queued. A battle that filled stays on the search and
says so. If FIND BATTLE hears nothing for five seconds, the search ends and
a box says the search could not be started.
