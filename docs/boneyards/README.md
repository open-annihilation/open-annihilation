# Boneyards

Notes for the built-in Boneyards module.

- [Help pages](help-pages.md) are the loose pages the game ships in place of the expansion's Help.
- [The galactic map](war-map.md) is the war map the client keeps, the Galactic day, and moving across it by keyboard.
- [Colour vision](colour-vision.md) is how a colour-vision mode recolours the war map, chat and player rows.
- [Settings](settings.md) are the Profile numbers, the release constants, and the master servers.
- [Decisions](decisions.md) record how the module is built into the engine.
- [Entry](entry.md) is how a player comes in from the main menu.
- [A side for this sector](allegiance.md) is the login prompt and the Allegiance tab.
- [Matchmaking](matchmaking.md) is the queue that finds a battle.
- [QuickPlay](quickplay.md) is the search that finds a forming battle, or hosts one.
- [Typing](typing.md) is which field has the keyboard when a console opens.
- [Controls](../controls.md), under The original menus, is the main menu's Tab order, the strip's Enter and Credits included.
- [Engine follow-ups](engine-follow-ups.md) are behaviours the engine still owes the module.
- After a battle that reached the game, these screens say a ladder battle goes on Core Prime by itself. A recorded For Fun battle offers Share… and Not now. Share… leaves these screens and opens the Library on that recording with the share question, after Sign in when the player is signed out. A For Fun battle that was not recorded says so and does not ask to share. A launch that never reached the game, the battle room's back button and a failed join say nothing. The Library does the upload. Open on Core Prime is on a listed battle's card whether or not the player is signed in. Share my own recording waits until they are.
