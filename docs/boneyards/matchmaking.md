# Matchmaking

Matchmaking is the queue on the Main Frame. It is open to an admin or a beta
tester. Anyone else does not see it.

The dialog asks for a game, a skill band, a side and a planet, then shows how
many commanders are already queued. ACTIVATE joins that queue. The search
keeps its clock when the filters change. Close hides the dialog and leaves
the search running: MATCHMAKING blinks until the dialog is opened again or
DEACTIVATE leaves the queue. Opening the settings face while a search is
running does not leave it.

When an opponent is found, the dialog comes back on the searching face with
Yes and No. Yes accepts the battle and stays on the search until it starts.
No refuses it and stays in the queue. The offer closes either way.

A search that the queue will not take returns to the settings face and says
why: the account cannot queue, the battle is not allowed on that planet, the
commander is already in a battle, Fuel Dump is not in this sector, the queue
dropped the search, or the settings cannot be queued. A battle that filled
stays on the search and says so. If a join hears nothing for five seconds,
the search ends and a box says the queue is unavailable. Closing the dialog
does not cancel that wait.

The battle that starts is hosted or joined the same way as a battle from the
battle list. The side the queue assigns is remembered for the battle room.
