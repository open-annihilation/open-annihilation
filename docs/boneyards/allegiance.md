# A side for this sector

A registered member has a side for the sector they are logged into. The
dossier names it Arm or Core. A sector they have not chosen for yet has no
side. The side from the previous sector is remembered, and it is not the
side for this one until they choose it again.

When the dossier has been read and the side is unset, the login stays up
and a prompt asks for Arm or Core before the Main Frame takes input. The
prompt names the last sector's side, when there was one, and that side
starts selected. Choose stores the side. The Main Frame opens once the
dossier comes back naming Arm or Core. EXIT leaves the Boneyards. Nothing
else dismisses the prompt. A guest is not asked.

The prompt is a Boneyards message box, the size of its picture, in the
middle of the console. The console around it is empty: the login's
controls are covered while the prompt is up, and a click outside the box
does nothing. Inside the box the message is wrapped above Arm and Core,
each with its insignia, on one row, and Choose and EXIT sit on the bottom
row. The message fits inside the box in English and in each language the
Boneyards' words are translated into.

On the Allegiance tab the Arm and Core choices stay available, and changing
one stores the new side, until the first factional game. After that game
the tab shows the side and the choices cannot be changed.
