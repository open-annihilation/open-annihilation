# A full battle room

The battle room seats ten players. A player who arrives while every seat
is taken stays in the game. When a seat opens, because someone leaves or
a closed seat is opened, the player who has been waiting longest is seated
there. Each player who arrived after them follows, in that order. A player
who leaves while waiting is not seated.

A player the host refuses or rejects is told why and leaves. A seat that
opens after that does not seat them. Once the game starts, a player still
waiting is not seated.

While a seat is free, a player who arrives takes it at once. The seat they
take, and the messages the room sends, are the ones sent for any arrival.
The map and the options a waiting player had already sent are there when
they sit down.
